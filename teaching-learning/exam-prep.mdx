Tianiazy includes a dedicated exam preparation module with content aligned to Madagascar's national examinations — CEPE (primary), BEPC (lower secondary), and Bac (upper secondary). Students can practise at their own pace, and teachers can assign targeted revision directly from the platform.

## Accessing Exam Prep

- **Students** — go to **Study > Exam Prep** in your workspace.
- **Teachers** — access and assign prep content from **Teaching > Exam Prep**.

## Available Content

| Exam | Level | Subjects Available |
|------|-------|--------------------|
| **CEPE** | Fin de primaire (CM2) | Malagasy, French, Math, Sciences, History & Geography |
| **BEPC** | Fin de collège (3ème) | Malagasy, French, Math, Physics, Biology, History & Geography, Civic Education |
| **Bac (Séries A, C, D)** | Fin de lycée (Terminale) | Depends on series; full subject coverage available |

## Practice Questions

Work through practice questions step by step:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an exam">
    From **Study > Exam Prep**, select the exam you are preparing for (CEPE, BEPC, or Bac).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a subject">
    Choose the subject you want to practise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a year or topic">
    Filter by a specific exam year or by topic to focus your revision session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the questions">
    Work through each question in the interactive question panel. You can navigate between questions freely before submitting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for scoring">
    Click **Submit** when you finish. Your score is calculated immediately and added to your progress record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Past Papers

Tianiazy provides downloadable PDF past examination papers organised by year and subject. To access them, go to **Exam Prep > Past Papers**, choose the exam and subject, then select the year. Click **Download** to save the paper for offline study.

## Assigning Prep to Students

As a teacher, you can assign any exam prep content as homework:

1. Go to **Teaching > Exam Prep** and browse to the content you want to assign.
2. Click **Assign to Class**, select the target class, and set a due date.
3. The task appears in each student's **Homework** list with the **Exam Prep** badge, making it easy to distinguish from regular homework.

<Tip>
  Students can track their practice scores over time from **Study > My Progress**.
</Tip>

<Info>
  Content is reviewed annually and updated after each national exam cycle.
</Info>
