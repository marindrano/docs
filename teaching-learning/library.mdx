Tianiazy's library module lets librarians catalog the school's book collection, manage borrowing, and track overdue returns. Students and staff can search available titles in real time, and the platform keeps copy counts accurate automatically.

## Adding Books

Add each title to the catalog so students and staff can find it in search results. You only need to do this once per title — Tianiazy tracks all copies under a single catalog record.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Book form">
    Go to **Library > Catalog > Add Book**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the book details">
    Fill in the following fields:
    - **Title** and **Author**
    - **ISBN** (optional but recommended for easy identification)
    - **Subject category** (e.g., Mathematics, Literature, Sciences)
    - **Available copies** — the number of physical copies in your library
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the record">
    Click **Save**. The book appears immediately in the searchable catalog for students and staff.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Borrowing a Book

<Steps>
  <Step title="Student or staff submits a request">
    The borrower goes to **Library > Search** in their workspace, finds the book, and clicks **Request Loan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Librarian opens a new loan">
    Go to **Library > Loans > New Loan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the borrower and book">
    Type the student's or staff member's name and the book title to populate the loan form. Set the **return due date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the loan">
    Click **Confirm**. The available copy count is decremented automatically and the borrower is notified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Returning a Book

Go to **Library > Loans > Active** and find the loan you want to close. Click **Return** next to the record. Tianiazy marks the loan as returned and increments the available copy count immediately — no manual adjustment needed.

## Overdue Notices

Tianiazy flags any loan whose return date has passed. To manage overdue items:

1. Go to **Library > Overdue** to see a list of all outstanding loans.
2. Review the borrower, book title, and number of days overdue.
3. Click **Send Reminder** to notify the borrower through the platform's internal messaging system. You can send reminders to individual borrowers or to all overdue borrowers at once.

## Student Search

Students search the full catalog from their workspace under **Resources > Library**. Each result shows the book title, author, subject category, and real-time availability (number of copies currently on the shelf). Students can request a loan directly from the search results without visiting the library desk.

<Note>
  Admins can set borrowing limits per role — for example, students can borrow up to 2 books and staff up to 5 — from **Settings > Library**.
</Note>
