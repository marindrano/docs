Tianiazy lets teachers assign homework and build online quizzes that students complete from their workspace or mobile app. All assignments and results are stored in one place, keeping students, parents, and teachers aligned throughout the school year.

## Assigning Homework

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Assignment form">
    Go to **Teaching > Homework > New Assignment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your class and subject">
    Choose the target class and the subject the assignment belongs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write instructions and attach files">
    Enter your instructions in the text editor. Attach any supporting files — PDFs, images, or documents — using the attachment button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the due date">
    Pick the date and time by which students must submit their work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the assignment">
    Click **Publish**. Students receive an immediate notification in their workspace and on the mobile app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Submitting Homework (Students)

To submit a homework assignment, go to **Homework > My Assignments** in your student workspace. Open the task you want to complete, then either upload your work (documents, images, PDFs) or type your response directly in the text box. When you are ready, click **Submit**. Your teacher is notified automatically.

## Reviewing Submissions

Open **Teaching > Homework** and select the assignment. You see each student's submission status at a glance:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Submitted** | The student has submitted on time |
| **Pending** | The student has not yet submitted |
| **Late** | The student submitted after the due date |

Click any student's row to open their submission. You can read or download their work, leave a written comment, and enter a grade. The grade is saved to the student's record immediately.

## Creating Quizzes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Quiz form">
    Go to **Teaching > Quizzes > New Quiz**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your questions">
    Add as many questions as you need. Tianiazy supports three question types:
    - **Multiple choice** — provide 2–6 answer options and mark the correct one
    - **True / False** — students choose between two options
    - **Short answer** — students type a free-text response
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure timing">
    Set a **time limit** (how long students have once they start) and an **available window** (the start and end datetime during which the quiz can be taken).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to a class">
    Select the target class and click **Publish**. Students see the quiz in their workspace as soon as it opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quiz Results

Tianiazy calculates grades automatically for **multiple-choice** and **true/false** questions as soon as a student submits. **Short-answer** questions require manual marking — open **Teaching > Quizzes**, select the quiz, and score each response individually.

Once graded, results appear directly in the student's gradebook. Students can review their score and see which questions they answered correctly.

<Tip>
  Students can take quizzes on mobile. Results sync instantly when they submit.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Parents can see homework status and quiz scores from their workspace.
</Note>
