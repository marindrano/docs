Tianiazy is a fully integrated school management platform designed specifically for schools in Madagascar. From the moment a student applies for admission to the day they graduate, Tianiazy handles every administrative and academic workflow in one place. The platform runs entirely in the cloud on Cloudflare's global network, ensuring fast, reliable access whether you're in Antananarivo or a remote region. Every feature is available in both **French and English**, mobile money payments are built in (MVola, Orange Money, Airtel Money), and exam preparation content is aligned to Madagascar's national exams — CEPE, BEPC, and Bac.

## What's Included

Tianiazy ships with a comprehensive set of modules that cover every aspect of school operations. Activate only what your school needs — all modules are configurable from **Settings > Modules**.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Admissions" icon="file-pen">
    Manage online and walk-in applications, review documents, and enroll students into classes with a structured workflow.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Attendance" icon="calendar-check">
    Record and track daily attendance by class or subject. Automated alerts notify parents of absences in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Timetables" icon="clock">
    Build and publish class timetables, assign teachers to subjects, and handle room allocation without conflicts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Grades & Reports" icon="chart-bar">
    Enter marks, compute averages, apply your grading scale, and generate official report cards in PDF format.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Homework & Quizzes" icon="pencil">
    Teachers assign homework and create online quizzes. Students submit work digitally and receive feedback instantly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Exam Prep" icon="graduation-cap">
    Curated past papers and practice exercises aligned to CEPE, BEPC, and Bac national examination syllabi.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fee Collection" icon="money-bill-wave">
    Issue invoices, accept payments via MVola, Orange Money, or Airtel Money, and reconcile accounts automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payroll" icon="briefcase">
    Calculate teacher and staff salaries, apply deductions, and generate payslips on a monthly or custom schedule.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Library" icon="book">
    Catalog books and resources, manage borrowing and returns, and let students search the collection online.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Canteen & Transport" icon="bus">
    Track meal plans and canteen balances, manage bus routes, and assign students to transport schedules.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dormitories" icon="bed">
    Allocate rooms, track occupancy, manage check-in and check-out, and monitor boarding student welfare.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Messaging & Website" icon="envelope">
    Send direct messages between all roles, post school-wide announcements, and publish your school's public website with the built-in website builder.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Tianiazy supports **French and English** out of the box. Every user — admin, teacher, student, or parent — can independently choose their preferred language from their profile settings. All system notifications, report cards, and invoices respect the user's selected language.
</Note>
