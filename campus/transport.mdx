Tianiazy's transport module helps schools manage bus routes, assign students, and collect transport fees through the platform — giving administrators, parents, and drivers all the information they need in one place.

## Creating Routes

Set up each bus route with its stops, schedule, vehicle, and driver before assigning any students.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Route form">
    Go to **Transport > Routes > New Route**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter route details">
    Enter the **route name**, list the **stops in order**, select the **assigned vehicle**, and choose the **driver** from your staff directory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set pickup and drop-off times">
    Enter the **morning pickup time** for the first stop and the **afternoon drop-off time** for the last stop. You can add individual stop times if your schedule varies by stop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the route">
    Click **Save**. The route is now available for student assignments and visible to relevant staff.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning Students

Once a route exists, you can assign students to it directly.

Go to **Transport > Students** and search for the student by name or ID. Select the student, choose their route from the dropdown, and confirm their boarding stop. Click **Save** — the assignment is visible to the parent immediately in their workspace. You can assign multiple students in sequence without leaving the page.

<Note>
  You can update a student's route mid-year from their profile under the **Transport** tab. Changes take effect the following school day.
</Note>

## Transport Fees

Transport fees are tied to routes and posted automatically to student accounts.

Set a monthly transport fee for each route from **Transport > Fees**. Enter the route and the corresponding monthly amount, then save. Fees are posted to the student's account at the start of each month and appear in **Finance > Invoices** alongside other school charges. If a student changes routes, the fee is updated from the following billing period.

<Info>
  Transport fees support the same payment methods as other school fees, including mobile money payments via MVola, Orange Money, and Airtel Money.
</Info>

## Route Information for Parents

Parents can access up-to-date transport details from their workspace at any time.

Under the **Transport** section of their workspace, parents see their child's assigned route, boarding stop, and scheduled pickup and drop-off times. This reduces the volume of transport-related inquiries to the school office and ensures parents are never caught off guard by schedule changes.

<Tip>
  Export the full passenger list for each route from **Transport > Routes > Export as PDF** — useful for drivers to verify who should be on board before departure.
</Tip>
