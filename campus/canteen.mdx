Tianiazy's canteen module lets schools manage daily meal plans, track which students are eating, and bill canteen fees to student accounts automatically — keeping nutrition records and finances in one place.

## Setting Up Menus

Create a menu for each school day so that subscribed students and parents always know what is being served.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Menu form">
    Go to **Canteen > Menus > New Menu**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter date and meal details">
    Enter the date, then fill in the details for each meal period: **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, and **Afternoon Snack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the price per meal">
    Enter the price for each meal period. You can set different prices for breakfast, lunch, and snack if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the menu">
    Click **Save**. The menu is immediately visible to subscribed students and their parents in their respective workspaces.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Student Subscriptions

Enroll students in canteen plans so their consumption is tracked and billed automatically.

From **Canteen > Subscriptions**, search for a student and open their subscription settings. Choose a plan frequency — **daily**, **weekly**, or **monthly** — based on how the student will use the canteen. Once you save the subscription, fees are posted to the student's account automatically according to the plan selected.

You can update or cancel a subscription at any time from the same screen. Changes to the subscription type take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## Daily Attendance

Accurate daily attendance is the foundation of fair canteen billing.

Canteen staff mark which students ate each day from **Canteen > Today's Attendance**. The attendance list is pre-populated from active subscriptions, so staff only need to confirm or remove students who were absent. This attendance record feeds directly into the billing engine, ensuring students are only charged for meals they actually received.

## Billing

Canteen fees are calculated based on recorded attendance, so students are never overbilled for missed meals.

Accountants can view, review, and reconcile all canteen charges from **Finance > Canteen Fees**. Each charge entry shows the student name, date, meal type, and amount. You can filter by class, date range, or billing status to generate reports or identify outstanding balances.

<Info>
  Canteen fees appear on the student's account alongside other school fees such as tuition and transport, giving parents and accountants a unified view of all outstanding amounts.
</Info>

## Parent Visibility

Parents stay informed about their child's canteen activity without needing to contact the school directly.

From their workspace, parents can view their child's current canteen balance, a log of recent meals consumed, and any pending canteen charges. This transparency reduces billing disputes and encourages timely top-ups for prepaid plans.

<Tip>
  Use the **Canteen Dashboard** to see a daily headcount and manage last-minute changes — for example, removing a student who called in sick after the kitchen has already started preparing meals.
</Tip>
