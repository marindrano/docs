Tianiazy's dormitory module lets schools manage boarding accommodations — from room assignments to fee billing and attendance tracking — so that supervisors, accountants, and parents all have real-time visibility into boarding life.

## Setting Up Dormitories

Before assigning any students, configure your buildings and rooms so the system accurately reflects your physical infrastructure.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new building">
    Go to **Dormitories > Buildings > New Building**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter building details">
    Enter the **building name** and the **total number of rooms** it contains.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure each room">
    For each room, set its **capacity** (maximum number of students) and its **room type** — either **Shared** or **Private**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the building">
    Click **Save**. The building and its rooms are now available for student assignments and appear in the occupancy overview.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning Students to Rooms

Assign boarding students to specific rooms once your buildings are configured.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Room Assignments">
    Go to **Dormitories > Room Assignments**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the student">
    Search for the student by name or ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a room">
    Choose an available room from the list. Rooms that have reached capacity are greyed out and cannot be selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the check-in date">
    Enter the student's **check-in date** to record when boarding begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the assignment">
    Click **Save**. The room's occupancy count is updated immediately, and the assignment is reflected in the occupancy overview.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Boarding Fees

Boarding fees are calculated per room type and posted to student accounts automatically each month.

Set the monthly fee for each room type from **Dormitories > Fee Structures**. Enter the room type (Shared or Private) and the corresponding monthly amount, then save. Fees post automatically to each boarding student's account at the start of every month and appear in **Finance > Invoices** alongside tuition and other charges.

<Info>
  If a student changes rooms mid-month — for example, moving from a shared room to a private one — you can adjust their fee structure from their room assignment record. The new fee applies from the following month.
</Info>

## Occupancy Overview

Monitor the current state of all dormitory buildings from a single screen.

From **Dormitories > Overview**, you see a summary of every building with each room's current occupancy versus its maximum capacity. Rooms that exceed capacity are **highlighted in red**, allowing supervisors to identify and resolve overcrowding quickly. Use this view at the start of each term to confirm that all assignments are in order before boarding begins.

<Warning>
  Never manually override capacity limits by editing room records directly. Use the Room Assignments screen, which enforces capacity rules and prevents accidental overbooking.
</Warning>

## Boarding Attendance

Nightly check-in records ensure that all boarding students are accounted for.

Boarding supervisors record nightly check-ins from **Dormitories > Attendance**. The attendance list is pre-populated with all students currently assigned to a room, so supervisors only need to mark absences. When a student is marked absent, the system flags the record and automatically sends a notification to the relevant administrators so that appropriate action can be taken promptly.

<Note>
  Parents of boarding students can see their child's room assignment and boarding fee balance from their workspace, keeping them informed without requiring them to contact the school directly.
</Note>
