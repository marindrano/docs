This quickstart walks you through the essential steps to get your school fully operational on Tianiazy. It is designed for **school administrators** setting up Tianiazy for the first time. By the end, you will have a live school profile, active staff accounts, and at least one enrolled student — all ready to use the platform's full feature set.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to [app.tianiazy.com](https://app.tianiazy.com) and click **Get Started**. Fill in the registration form with the following details:

    - **School Name** — enter the official name of your institution.
    - **Country** — select **Madagascar** from the dropdown.
    - **Language** — choose **French** or **English** as your school's default language. You can change this later.
    - **Administrator Email** — use an email address you have access to; this becomes your login.
    - **Password** — choose a strong password of at least 8 characters.

    After submitting the form, check your inbox for a confirmation email and click the **Verify Email** link. Your school account is now active.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Your School Profile">
    Once you log in for the first time, you are taken to the **School Setup** wizard. Complete each section to prepare your school profile for daily use:

    - **Logo** — upload your school's official logo (PNG or JPG, minimum 200×200 px). It appears on report cards, invoices, and your school website.
    - **Academic Year** — set the start and end dates of the current academic year (for example, 1 September 2025 to 30 June 2026).
    - **Grading Scale** — define your grading system. You can use numeric scales (0–20, 0–100) or letter grades (A–F). Set passing thresholds per grade level if needed.
    - **Active Modules** — toggle on the modules your school uses. You can enable or disable any module later from **Settings > Modules**.

    Click **Save Profile** to confirm your configuration and proceed to the main dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite Staff">
    Navigate to **Users > Staff** in the left sidebar and click **Invite Staff Member**. For each staff member:

    1. Enter their **full name** and **email address**.
    2. Select their **role** from the dropdown:
       - **Admin** — full platform access and school settings.
       - **Teacher** — classroom management, grades, attendance, and homework.
       - **Accountant** — fee collection, payroll, and financial reports.
       - **Receptionist** — admissions, visitor log, and front-desk tools.
    3. Click **Send Invitation**.

    Each invited staff member receives an email with a secure login link. They set their own password on first login. You can invite multiple staff members at once by uploading a CSV file from the **Bulk Import** option on the same page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enroll Your First Students">
    Navigate to **Admissions > New Student** and complete the enrollment form:

    - **Personal Details** — full name, date of birth, gender, and national ID or birth certificate number.
    - **Contact Information** — home address and emergency contact.
    - **Class Assignment** — select the academic level and class section the student belongs to (for example, *Classe de 6ème — Section A*).
    - **Parent / Guardian** — enter the parent's name, phone number, and email. Tianiazy automatically creates a parent account and sends them a login invitation if you check **Notify Parent**.

    Click **Enroll Student** to confirm. The student now appears on the class register, and their parent (if notified) can log in to the family workspace immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can switch between **French and English** at any time from the language selector in the top navigation bar. Your preference is saved automatically and applies across all pages, notifications, and generated documents.
</Tip>
