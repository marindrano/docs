Tianiazy's payroll module lets accountants configure salary components, run monthly payroll, and distribute payslips to staff — all without leaving the platform.

## Setting Up Salary Structures

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a New Salary Structure">
    Go to **Payroll > Salary Structures > New**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Salary Components">
    Add the components that make up the salary package. Typical components include:
    - **Base Salary**
    - **Transport Allowance**
    - **Housing Allowance**
    - **CNAPS Contribution**
    - **IRSA** (income tax)

    Set each component as an earning or a deduction and enter the amount or percentage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the Structure to Staff">
    Open a staff member's profile, go to the **Payroll** tab, and select the salary structure to assign to them. Repeat for each staff member.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Running Monthly Payroll

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Payroll Run Screen">
    Go to **Payroll > Run Payroll**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Period">
    Choose the **month** and **year** you want to process payroll for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Adjust">
    Tianiazy calculates each staff member's net pay automatically based on their assigned salary structure. Review the figures and adjust individual entries if needed — for example, to account for absences or bonuses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process Payroll">
    Click **Process** to finalize the payroll run. Payslips are generated immediately for all staff.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payslips

Once payroll is processed, each staff member can view and download their payslip by going to **My Payslip** in their workspace. Payslips are available as PDF and include a full breakdown of earnings and deductions for the selected period.

## Deductions and Advances

To record a salary advance, go to **Payroll > Advances**, select the staff member, and enter the advance amount. Tianiazy applies the deduction automatically in the next payroll cycle — you do not need to adjust the payroll manually. You can view the history of advances and deductions for any staff member from their profile's **Payroll** tab.

## Payroll Reports

Export monthly payroll summaries as **PDF** or **CSV** from **Payroll > Reports**. These reports are formatted to support tax filings and CNAPS declarations, saving you time at the end of each month.

<Note>
  Tianiazy calculates IRSA and CNAPS contributions automatically based on current Madagascar tax tables. Contact support if rates change so your tables can be updated promptly.
</Note>
