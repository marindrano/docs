Tianiazy integrates directly with Madagascar's three major mobile money networks — MVola (Telma), Orange Money, and Airtel Money — so parents can pay school fees without visiting the school.

## How Mobile Money Payments Work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Payment Screen">
    The parent logs into their Tianiazy workspace and goes to **Finance > Pay Fees**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Invoice">
    The parent selects the outstanding invoice they want to pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Mobile Money Provider">
    The parent chooses their mobile money provider: **MVola**, **Orange Money**, or **Airtel Money**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Mobile Number">
    The parent enters their registered mobile number and confirms the payment request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Push Notification">
    A push notification is sent to the parent's phone. The parent approves the transaction directly on their device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment Recorded Automatically">
    Once the transaction is confirmed by the provider, Tianiazy records the payment instantly and marks the invoice as **Paid**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported Providers

| Provider | Network | Currency |
|---|---|---|
| MVola | Telma Madagascar | MGA (Ariary) |
| Orange Money | Orange Madagascar | MGA (Ariary) |
| Airtel Money | Airtel Madagascar | MGA (Ariary) |

## Enabling Mobile Money

To activate mobile money for your school, go to **Settings > Finance > Mobile Money**. Toggle on each provider you want to accept, then enter your school's **merchant account details** for that provider. Repeat the process for each provider you wish to enable. Changes take effect immediately once you save.

## Reconciliation

All mobile money transactions appear in **Finance > Transactions**, including the provider's reference number for each payment. To reconcile your mobile money income against your bank or merchant statements, go to **Finance > Reconciliation** and match transactions by date range or provider.

<Note>
  Mobile money payments are confirmed in real time. If a payment fails, the invoice remains **Outstanding** and the parent is notified automatically.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Ensure your merchant account details are correct before enabling a provider. Incorrect details will cause payment failures.
</Warning>
