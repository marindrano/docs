Tianiazy's fee collection module lets accountants configure fee structures, issue invoices, record payments, and generate financial reports — all from one dashboard.

## Setting Up Fee Structures

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fee Structures">
    Go to **Finance > Fee Structures > New**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Fee Details">
    Enter the fee name (e.g., *School Fees Trimester 1*), the amount, and the applicable classes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Due Date">
    Choose the due date by which students must pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Fee Structure">
    Click **Save**. The fee is added automatically to every matching student account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Bulk fee posting:** Use **Finance > Fee Structures > Post to All** to apply a fee to every student in a class at once.
</Tip>

## Issuing Invoices

Go to **Finance > Invoices** to view all invoices. Invoices are generated automatically when you save a fee structure, so you do not need to create them one by one. If you need to charge a student for an individual item — such as a lost library book or a school trip — you can also create a manual invoice directly from the same page by clicking **New Invoice** and selecting the student.

## Recording Payments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Payment Form">
    Go to **Finance > Payments > Record Payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Student">
    Search for the student by name or ID number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Invoice">
    Choose the outstanding invoice you want to settle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Payment Method">
    Select one of the available payment methods: **Cash**, **Bank Transfer**, or **Mobile Money**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Payment Details">
    Enter the amount paid and the reference number (receipt number, bank transfer reference, or mobile money transaction ID), then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Outstanding Balances

View all unpaid invoices from **Finance > Balances**. Use the filters to narrow results by class or date range. When you are ready to share or archive the data, export the list as **CSV** or **PDF** using the **Export** button in the top-right corner.

## Payment Receipts

A receipt PDF is generated automatically after each payment is recorded. Parents can download their receipt at any time by logging into their Tianiazy workspace and going to **Finance > My Receipts**.
