The timetable module lets admins build weekly schedules, assign teachers to every period, and publish the final timetable so that all users — teachers, students, and parents — can view it instantly from their own workspace. You can manage multiple classes and academic years side by side, and Tianiazy flags any conflicts the moment they arise.

## Creating a Timetable

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a New Timetable">
    Go to **Timetables > New Timetable**. Give the timetable a name (for example, "6ème A — Trimestre 1 2025") so you can identify it easily.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Class and Academic Year">
    Choose the class this timetable applies to and the academic year it belongs to. Each class can have one active published timetable per academic year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Periods">
    Use the drag-and-drop grid to place subjects into timeslots. For each subject block:
    - Drag the subject from the sidebar onto the day and time you want.
    - Assign a teacher from the dropdown that appears on the block.
    - Optionally assign a classroom or room number.

    Repeat until all required periods are filled for every day of the week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for Conflicts">
    As you build the timetable, Tianiazy highlights any double-bookings in **red** — for example, if the same teacher is scheduled in two classes at the same time, or the same room is assigned to two groups simultaneously. Resolve all conflicts before publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the Timetable">
    Click **Publish**. Students see the updated schedule immediately under **Schedule** in their workspace, and teachers see it under **My Timetable**. You can unpublish and edit at any time, then republish to push the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing Your Timetable

Once a timetable is published, every user can access it from their own workspace:

| Role | Where to find the timetable |
|---|---|
| **Teacher** | **My Timetable** — shows only the periods assigned to that teacher |
| **Student** | **Schedule** — shows the full weekly timetable for their class |
| **Parent** | **Child's Schedule** in the parent workspace |
| **Admin** | **Timetables** — full view of all class timetables |

## Substitutions

When a teacher is absent, you can assign a substitute without rebuilding the entire timetable.

1. Go to **Timetables > Substitutions**.
2. Select the date and the period(s) affected.
3. Choose the absent teacher.
4. Select the substitute teacher from the list of available staff (Tianiazy only shows teachers who are free during that period).
5. Click **Assign**. The substitute receives an automatic notification with the class, room, and subject details.

The substitution is recorded in the timetable log and is visible to students in their schedule for that day, clearly marked as a substitution.

## Timetable Export

To share the timetable outside the platform:

1. Go to **Timetables > Export**.
2. Select the class and the week or full term you want to export.
3. Click **Export as PDF**.

The generated PDF is formatted for A4 printing and is ready to post on classroom notice boards or distribute at parent meetings.

<Tip>
  Tianiazy checks for room and teacher conflicts in real time as you build the timetable. Save time by resolving conflicts as you go rather than waiting until you are ready to publish — the red highlights update instantly with every change you make.
</Tip>
