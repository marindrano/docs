The attendance module lets teachers and admins record presence, lateness, and absences per class period or for the full school day. All records are stored in real time, feeding automatically into absence reports and parent notifications so nothing falls through the cracks.

## Student Attendance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Today's Attendance">
    Go to **Attendance > Today**. You see a list of all classes scheduled for the current day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Class">
    Click the class you are recording attendance for. The full student roster loads automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark Each Student">
    For every student, select one of the three statuses:
    - **Present** — student is in class
    - **Absent** — student did not attend
    - **Late** — student arrived after the period started

    You can add a short remark to any individual record (for example, "left early — medical appointment").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Register">
    Click **Save**. The register is locked for that period. If a student is marked **Absent**, the system immediately triggers a notification to their parent or guardian.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Parent notifications are sent via the platform's built-in messaging system. If your school has SMS integration configured, parents also receive a text message at the number registered in their profile.
</Info>

## Staff Attendance

Admins record staff attendance from **Attendance > Staff**. The staff roster lists all active employees across all departments.

For each staff member, you can log:

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **Present** | Staff member is on-site and working |
| **Absent** | Staff member did not report for duty |
| **Late** | Staff member arrived after the scheduled start time |
| **Leave — Sick** | Absence covered by a medical leave entitlement |
| **Leave — Annual** | Planned holiday or annual leave |
| **Leave — Unpaid** | Absence taken without pay |

Leave records feed directly into the payroll module so that deductions and entitlements are calculated correctly at the end of the month.

## Attendance Reports

Generate detailed attendance reports at any time from **Attendance > Reports**.

- **Filter by class** — view attendance for a single class or across all classes
- **Filter by date range** — choose any start and end date
- **Filter by student** — pull the full attendance history for one individual

Once you have applied your filters, click **Generate Report**. You can export the result as a **PDF** for printing or as a **CSV** for further analysis in a spreadsheet.

## Absence Justifications

When a student is absent, their parent or the student themselves can submit a written justification directly from their Tianiazy workspace.

1. The parent or student goes to **Attendance > Justify Absence**.
2. They select the relevant absence date(s) and write an explanation.
3. They optionally attach a supporting document (doctor's note, official letter).
4. The admin or class teacher receives a notification and can mark the justification as **Approved** or **Rejected** from **Attendance > Justifications**.

Approved justifications are recorded against the absence and appear in reports so that the distinction between unjustified and justified absences is always clear.

<Note>
  Teachers can mark attendance from the Tianiazy mobile app while offline — data syncs automatically to the server as soon as the internet connection is restored. This is particularly useful in areas with intermittent connectivity.
</Note>
