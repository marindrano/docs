The Admin workspace gives you full access to every Tianiazy module — from student admissions and staff management to finance, the school website, and exam preparation. As an administrator, you are the central authority for configuring the platform, managing users across all roles, and keeping a bird's-eye view of your school's daily operations.

## Your Dashboard

When you log in, the Admin dashboard gives you an immediate snapshot of your school:

- **Total Students** — the current number of enrolled students across all classes.
- **Staff** — the total number of active staff members on the platform.
- **Outstanding Fees** — the cumulative unpaid fee balance across all students.
- **Today's Attendance Rate** — the percentage of students marked present for the current day.

Below the stats, the **Recent Activity Feed** shows the latest actions taken across the platform — new admissions submitted, payments recorded, grades published, and more. Use the **Quick Action Buttons** to jump directly to the most common tasks: add a student, record a payment, or send a broadcast message.

## System Settings

Use the **Settings** section to configure the platform for your school's specific needs. Key settings include:

- **Academic Year** — define the start and end dates of each academic year and manage terms or semesters.
- **Grading Scales** — set up custom grading systems (numeric, letter-grade, or competency-based) that apply across all classes.
- **Roles & Permissions** — control exactly what each role (Admin, Teacher, Accountant, Receptionist, Student, Parent) can see and do.
- **Mobile Money** — connect and configure MVola, Orange Money, and Airtel Money for fee collection.
- **Notification Templates** — customize the SMS and email notifications sent to parents, students, and staff.
- **Languages** — switch the platform interface between French and English, or configure per-user language preferences.

## Reports

Generate school-wide insights from **Reports > Dashboard**. The following reports are available to you:

- **Attendance Report** — daily, weekly, or monthly attendance breakdowns by class or individual student.
- **Grade Report** — assessment results and averages across subjects and classes, including exam preparation performance for CEPE, BEPC, and Bac cycles.
- **Financial Summary** — total billed, collected, and outstanding fees for any date range.
- **Payroll Summary** — a consolidated view of all staff salary payments for a given period.
- **Enrollment Report** — student intake numbers by class, gender, or academic year.

All reports can be exported as PDF or CSV for sharing with your school board or administrative team.
