The Receptionist workspace focuses on front-desk tasks — welcoming visitors, processing admission inquiries, and being the first point of contact for parents and students. Your workspace gives you quick access to the tools you need to keep the front office running smoothly without exposing sensitive administrative or financial data.

## Key Tasks

| Task | Where to Find It |
|------|-----------------|
| Register a new admission inquiry | Admissions > New Inquiry |
| Search for a student | Search bar (top navigation) |
| Record a visitor | Visitors > New Visit |
| Send a message to staff | Messages > New Message |
| View today's schedule | Timetables > Today |

## Admissions Intake

When a family arrives or calls to ask about enrollment, you create the initial record in Tianiazy so that no inquiry is lost.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new inquiry">
    Go to **Admissions > New Inquiry** and fill in the student's name, date of birth, desired class level, and the parent or guardian's contact details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record any supporting notes">
    Add any relevant context — preferred start date, special needs, sibling already enrolled — in the **Notes** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Click **Submit Inquiry**. The inquiry is automatically forwarded to the admissions team, who will review it, contact the family, and move it through the enrollment pipeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you submit an inquiry, your role in the process is complete. The admissions team handles all follow-up steps, from document collection to final enrollment confirmation.

## Visitor Log

Record every visitor who enters the school premises to maintain a clear and auditable log of on-site activity.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new visit">
    Go to **Visitors > New Visit** and enter the visitor's full name, contact number, reason for visit, and the name of the staff member they are there to see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue a visitor pass">
    Once the record is saved, Tianiazy generates a visit entry. Note the visitor's arrival time — this is recorded automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log checkout">
    When the visitor leaves, open their record in **Visitors** and click **Check Out** to record their departure time and close the visit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A complete visitor record includes name, contact, reason for visit, the staff member being visited, arrival time, and checkout time. Make sure all fields are filled before saving.
</Note>

## Student Search

Use the **Search bar in the top navigation** to look up any student instantly without navigating through class lists.

- Type the student's **full name** or **student ID** to find their profile.
- From the search result, you can view their current class, parent contact information, and outstanding fee balance.
- You cannot edit student profiles from the Receptionist workspace — if a correction is needed, contact your school administrator.

<Tip>
  Keep the Visitor Log open in a pinned browser tab for quick access throughout the day.
</Tip>
