The Teacher workspace puts everything you need to manage your classes in one place — from daily attendance to grades, homework, quizzes, and parent communication. Whether you're in the classroom or on the go, Tianiazy keeps your teaching organised and your students connected.

## Your Dashboard

When you log in, your dashboard gives you an immediate overview of your day:

- **Today's classes** — your scheduled lessons pulled directly from your timetable
- **Pending homework to grade** — assignments submitted by students that are waiting for your review
- **Recent messages** — the latest messages from students, parents, or colleagues
- **Class attendance summary** — a quick view of attendance rates across your classes

## Daily Workflow

Follow these steps to stay on top of your responsibilities each school day:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check today's timetable">
    Open **My Timetable** to see which classes you have, their times, and their rooms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take attendance for each class">
    Go to **Attendance > Today** and mark each student present, absent, or late before or at the start of each lesson.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review submitted homework">
    Navigate to **Teaching > Homework > Review** to open submitted assignments, leave feedback, and record marks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to messages">
    Open **Messages** to reply to any incoming messages from students, parents, or administration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Tasks

Use this table to find any feature quickly:

| Task | Where to Find It |
|---|---|
| Take attendance | Attendance > Today |
| Enter grades | Grades > Grade Entry |
| Assign homework | Teaching > Homework > New |
| Create a quiz | Teaching > Quizzes > New |
| View timetable | My Timetable |
| Message a parent | Messages > New Message |
| Access exam prep content | Teaching > Exam Prep |

## Mobile App

You don't need to be at a desk to keep up with your classes. The Tianiazy mobile app lets you:

- **Take attendance** directly from your phone at the start of each lesson
- **Enter grades** as soon as assessments are complete
- **Read and reply to messages** from students and parents on the go

All changes sync instantly across devices, so your records are always up to date whether you use the app or the web platform.

<Tip>
  You can leave private notes on a student's profile — visible only to staff — from their profile page under the **Notes** tab. Use this to record pastoral observations, learning support needs, or any context that helps colleagues support the student.
</Tip>

<Note>
  If a student is marked absent, parents are automatically notified by the platform. You don't need to contact them separately.
</Note>
