The Parent workspace keeps you connected to your child's school life — view their attendance, grades, homework, and fee balance, and message teachers directly. You have a real-time window into how your child is doing without needing to visit the school or make phone calls.

## Getting Access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive your invitation">
    When your child is enrolled, the school sends an email invitation to the address you provided during registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password">
    Click the link in the email and follow the prompts to create your password and activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in or download the app">
    Sign in at [app.tianiazy.com](https://app.tianiazy.com) in your browser, or download the Tianiazy mobile app on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View all your children">
    If you have multiple children enrolled at the same school, they all appear in your workspace. Switch between them using the child selector at the top of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard gives you an at-a-glance summary of your child's school day:

- **Today's attendance status** — whether your child has been marked present, absent, or late
- **Recent grades** — the latest marks published by teachers
- **Upcoming fees due** — any outstanding payments approaching their due date
- **Pending messages** — unread messages from teachers or school administration

## Key Tasks

| Task | Where to Find It |
|---|---|
| View attendance | My Child > Attendance |
| Check grades | My Child > Grades |
| Download report card | My Child > Documents |
| See homework | My Child > Homework |
| Check fee balance | Finance > Fees |
| Pay fees (mobile money) | Finance > Pay Fees |
| Message a teacher | Messages > New Message |

## Paying Fees

Tianiazy lets you pay school fees directly from your phone using mobile money — no need to visit the school cashier.

To make a payment:

1. Open **Finance > Pay Fees**
2. Select the outstanding invoice you want to settle
3. Choose your mobile money provider: **MVola**, **Orange Money**, or **Airtel Money**
4. Enter your mobile number associated with your mobile money account
5. Confirm the push notification sent to your phone by your provider

Once the payment is confirmed, the invoice is marked as paid and a receipt is available under **Finance > Receipts**.

## Absence Justifications

If your child was absent, you can submit an official justification directly through Tianiazy rather than writing a paper note.

Go to **My Child > Attendance**, find the absence you want to explain, and click **Submit Justification**. Write your explanation and attach any supporting document if required. The school will review your submission and approve or follow up as needed.

<Tip>
  Install the Tianiazy mobile app to receive instant notifications when your child's attendance is recorded or new grades are published. You'll always know what's happening at school, even on busy days.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Your child cannot see the messages you exchange with their teachers. Conversations in the parent–teacher messaging channel are private to you and the school's staff.
</Note>
