Your Tianiazy workspace is your personal learning hub — see your schedule, complete assignments, check your grades, and prepare for CEPE, BEPC, or Bac exams. Everything your school shares with you is in one place, accessible from any device.

## Your Dashboard

When you log in, your dashboard shows you what matters most right now:

- **Today's classes** — your lessons for the day with times and room numbers
- **Upcoming homework deadlines** — assignments due soon so you can plan your time
- **Recent grades** — the latest marks and feedback from your teachers
- **New messages** — messages from your teachers or school administration

## Daily Workflow

Use this routine each school day to stay on top of your classes, assignments, and grades:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check today's timetable">
    Open **Schedule** to see all your lessons for the day, including the subject, teacher, and room.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review upcoming homework">
    Go to **Homework > My Assignments** to see all pending assignments and their due dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit completed work">
    Open the assignment you want to hand in, write your response or upload your file, then click **Submit** to send it to your teacher.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check recent grades">
    Navigate to **Grades > My Grades** to view marks and any feedback your teachers have left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Practice exam questions">
    Go to **Study > Exam Prep** to work through practice questions and past papers aligned to your exam level.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Grades and Reports

View all your grades organised by subject and period from **Grades > My Grades**. You can see individual assessment marks as well as your overall average for each subject.

When your school publishes your report card, download it as a PDF from **Documents > Report Cards**. You can save or print it directly from that page.

## Homework Submission

To submit an assignment:

1. Open the assignment from **Homework > My Assignments**
2. Write your response in the text field, or upload a file — accepted formats include PDF, images, and documents
3. Click **Submit** before the deadline shown on the assignment

Once submitted, your teacher will review your work and post feedback and a mark when grading is complete.

## Exam Preparation

Tianiazy includes practice materials aligned to Madagascar's national exams — CEPE, BEPC, and Bac. Access practice questions and past papers from **Study > Exam Prep**. Filter by subject and exam level to focus on what you need.

Track how you're progressing over time from **Study > My Progress**. This shows your scores across practice sessions so you can identify subjects that need more attention.

## Library

Browse and request books from your school's library without going to the desk. Search the catalogue from **Resources > Library**, then submit a request for any title you need.

Your active loans — books you currently have checked out — are listed under **My Loans**, along with their return dates.

## Language

Tianiazy is available in both French and English. To switch languages, go to the top navigation bar, click your name, and select **Language**. Your preference is saved to your account.

<Tip>
  Enable push notifications on the Tianiazy mobile app to never miss a homework deadline or new grade. Go to your phone's notification settings and make sure Tianiazy notifications are turned on.
</Tip>
