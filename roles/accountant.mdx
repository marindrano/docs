The Accountant workspace centralizes all financial operations — fee billing, payment recording, mobile money reconciliation, payroll processing, and reporting. Every transaction you handle in Tianiazy is logged, auditable, and linked to the student or staff member it relates to.

## Finance Dashboard

When you open the Accountant workspace, the Finance Dashboard gives you a real-time view of your school's financial position:

- **Total Fees Billed This Month** — the cumulative amount invoiced to all students in the current billing period.
- **Total Collected** — the sum of all confirmed payments received this month.
- **Outstanding Balance** — the difference between billed and collected amounts, representing unpaid fees across the school.
- **Recent Transactions** — a live feed of the latest payments recorded, whether made in cash, bank transfer, or via mobile money (MVola, Orange Money, Airtel Money).

## Daily Tasks

| Task | Where to Find It |
|------|-----------------|
| Record a payment | Finance > Payments > Record |
| Check outstanding balances | Finance > Balances |
| Reconcile mobile money | Finance > Reconciliation |
| Issue a receipt | Finance > Payments > (auto-generated) |

<Info>
  Receipts are generated automatically as soon as you record a payment. You can print or email the receipt directly from the payment confirmation screen.
</Info>

## End-of-Month Checklist

Follow these steps at the close of each month to ensure your financial records are complete and accurate:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run payroll for all staff">
    Go to **Payroll > Run Payroll**, select the current month, review each staff member's salary, deductions, and allowances, then confirm and process the payroll batch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review outstanding fee balances">
    Go to **Finance > Balances** to see every student with an unpaid or partially paid balance. Flag accounts that are significantly overdue for follow-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconcile mobile money transactions">
    Go to **Finance > Reconciliation** and match incoming mobile money transfers (MVola, Orange Money, Airtel Money) against recorded payments. Resolve any discrepancies before closing the month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export financial summary report">
    Go to **Reports > Financial Summary**, set the date range to the current month, and export the report as PDF or CSV for your records and school leadership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and archive payslips">
    Go to **Payroll > Payslips**, select the current month, and download the full payslip batch. Archive the files according to your school's document retention policy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Financial Reports

Generate and export detailed financial reports from **Reports > Finance**. The following reports are available:

- **Monthly Income Report** — a breakdown of all income received during a given month, grouped by payment method and fee type.
- **Fee Collection by Class** — see which classes have the highest collection rates and identify where outstanding balances are concentrated.
- **Unpaid Invoices** — a list of all invoices that remain fully or partially unpaid, with student names, amounts due, and due dates.
- **Payroll Summary** — a consolidated record of all salary payments made to staff for a selected period.

All reports can be exported as PDF or CSV and printed directly from the browser.

<Note>
  Only users with the **Accountant** or **Admin** role can access financial data. Permissions are managed from **Settings > Roles**.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use **Finance > Balances > Export** to send a list of outstanding balances to class teachers for follow-up with parents.
</Tip>
