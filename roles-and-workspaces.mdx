Tianiazy uses a role-based workspace model: when you log in, the platform shows you exactly what is relevant to your job — nothing more, nothing less. A teacher sees their classes, gradebooks, and homework queue. A parent sees their child's report card and fee balance. An accountant sees invoices and payroll. This focused design reduces confusion, protects sensitive data, and helps every user work more efficiently from day one.

---

## Workspace Overview

| Role | Primary Workspace | Key Capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full platform access | School settings, all reports, user management, all modules |
| **Teacher** | Classroom workspace | Attendance, grades, homework, quizzes, timetable, messaging |
| **Student** | Learning workspace | Timetable, grades, homework, quizzes, exam prep, library, messaging |
| **Parent** | Family workspace | Child's grades, attendance, fee balance, homework, messaging |
| **Accountant** | Finance workspace | Fee collection, payroll, financial reports, mobile money |
| **Receptionist** | Front-desk workspace | Admissions, visitor log, student search, messaging |

## Language Settings

Every user controls their own language preference independently. Changing your language does not affect any other user's experience.

To update your language:

1. Click your **profile avatar** in the top-right corner of any page.
2. Select **Profile Settings**.
3. Under **Preferences**, choose **French** or **English** from the **Language** dropdown.
4. Click **Save**. The interface, notifications, and generated documents (report cards, invoices, payslips) update immediately.

Parents who receive SMS or email notifications about their child's attendance or fees will also receive those messages in their selected language.

## Assigning Roles

You can assign or change a user's role at any time from the Users section of the admin panel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Users section">
    Navigate to **Users > Staff** to manage staff roles, or **Users > Students** to manage student accounts. Use the search bar to find a specific person quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a user profile">
    Click on the user's **name** in the list to open their full profile. You see their contact details, current role, login history, and any linked accounts (for example, a student linked to a parent).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit">
    Click the **Edit** button in the top-right corner of the profile card. The profile fields become editable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a role from the dropdown">
    Locate the **Role** field and click the dropdown. Select the appropriate role from the list:
    - Admin
    - Teacher
    - Accountant
    - Receptionist
    - Student
    - Parent
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. The user's workspace updates immediately on their next page load or login. If you reduced their permissions, any restricted pages they had open will redirect them to their new dashboard automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Admins can create **custom roles** with fine-grained permissions from **Settings > Roles**. For example, you can create a "Senior Teacher" role that includes access to the admin timetable builder, or a "Bursar Assistant" role with read-only access to financial reports but no ability to issue refunds.
</Note>
