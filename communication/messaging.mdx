Tianiazy includes a built-in messaging system that connects every stakeholder — admins, teachers, students, parents, accountants, and receptionists — without needing external apps. All communication stays within the platform, keeping conversations organized, secure, and tied to your school community.

## Sending a Message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Messages">
    Click the **Messages** icon in the top navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a New Message">
    Click **New Message** to open the compose window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Your Recipient">
    Search for the recipient by name or role. Tianiazy will suggest matching users from your school.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose Your Message">
    Type your message in the text field. Optionally, attach a file by clicking the **paperclip** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send**. The recipient receives your message instantly in their inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Group Announcements

Admins and teachers can send announcements to an entire class or role group at once — no need to message each person individually.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a New Message">
    Click **New Message** from the Messages panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Recipients">
    Click **Select Recipients** and choose either **Class** or **Role Group** from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose and Send">
    Write your announcement, then click **Send**. Every member of the selected class or group receives it directly in their inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Group announcements are one-way by default. Recipients can reply individually, but their replies are not shared with the rest of the group.
</Info>

## Message Notifications

You receive an in-app notification badge on the **Messages** icon whenever a new message arrives. If you use the Tianiazy mobile app, you also receive a **push notification** on your device — even when the app is running in the background.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="In-App Notifications" icon="bell">
    A red badge appears on the Messages icon in the navigation bar each time you receive a new message.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Push Notifications" icon="mobile">
    Mobile app users get real-time push notifications for every incoming message, ensuring no communication is missed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Read Receipts

Tianiazy shows you the delivery and read status of every message you send:

- **Grey tick** — the message has been delivered to the recipient's inbox.
- **Blue tick** — the recipient has opened and read the message.

Read receipts update in real time, so you always know whether your message has been seen.

## Conversation History

All messages are stored permanently and are fully searchable. To find a past conversation:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Search">
    Navigate to **Messages > Search** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply Filters">
    Use the available filters to narrow down results by **date**, **sender**, or **keyword**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Conversation">
    Click any result to open the full conversation thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Messaging is role-aware: students cannot message external contacts, only users within their school on Tianiazy.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Admins can send school-wide broadcasts from **Messaging > Broadcast** — useful for urgent announcements.
</Tip>
