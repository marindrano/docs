Tianiazy includes a no-code school website builder that lets admins publish a professional public-facing site with school information, news, and an online admissions form. No developers or design tools required — everything is managed directly from your admin workspace.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Website Builder">
    Go to **Website > Builder** in your admin workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Template">
    Browse the template gallery and click on the design that best fits your school's identity. Click **Use This Template** to apply it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize Your Branding">
    Edit your school name, upload your logo, choose your brand colors, and fill in your contact details using the editor panel on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your Pages">
    Create the pages your visitors need: **Home**, **About**, **Admissions**, **News**, and **Contact**. Click **Add Page** from the Pages panel to add any additional pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish Your Site">
    Click **Publish**. Your website goes live immediately at **[schoolname].tianiazy.com**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Preview your website before publishing from **Website > Preview** to see how it looks on desktop and mobile.
</Tip>

## Managing Content

Keep your website up to date by adding news, photos, and controlling the admissions form directly from the admin workspace.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="News & Events" icon="newspaper">
    Add articles from **Website > News > New Article**. Published articles appear automatically on the website's homepage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gallery" icon="images">
    Upload photos from **Website > Gallery**. Images are displayed in a responsive grid on the public site.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Admissions Page" icon="file-pen">
    Toggle the online application form on or off from **Website > Pages > Admissions** — useful for opening or closing enrollment periods.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Custom Domain

You can point your own domain (for example, `www.yourschool.mg`) to your Tianiazy website instead of using the default subdomain.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Domain Settings">
    Go to **Website > Settings > Custom Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Domain">
    Type your custom domain (e.g., `www.yourschool.mg`) in the domain field and click **Add Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Your DNS Records">
    Follow the DNS instructions displayed on screen. Log in to your domain registrar and add the provided **CNAME** or **A record** to point your domain to Tianiazy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Propagation">
    DNS propagation can take up to **48 hours**. Once complete, your custom domain will load your Tianiazy school website.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You can verify your domain status at any time from **Website > Settings > Custom Domain**. Tianiazy will show a green checkmark once the domain is active.
</Info>

## SEO Settings

Help parents and students find your school online by configuring your site's search engine settings.

Navigate to **Website > Settings > SEO** and fill in the following fields:

- **Page Title** — the title that appears in search engine results and browser tabs.
- **Description** — a short summary of your school shown below the title in search results.
- **Social Preview Image** — the image displayed when someone shares your website link on social media.

Completing these fields improves your school's visibility on Google and other search engines.

## Multilingual Content

Each page on your school website can be published in both **French** and **Malagasy** to reach all families in your community.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a Page in the Editor">
    Go to **Website > Builder** and click on the page you want to translate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch Language">
    Use the **language toggle** in the page editor toolbar to switch between French and Malagasy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Translated Content">
    Type the translated version of each text block directly in the editor. Content for each language is saved independently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**. Visitors can switch between languages using the language selector on your live website.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Make sure you publish content in both languages before sharing your site publicly. Pages with missing translations will display the default language content as a fallback.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The school website is hosted on Cloudflare, ensuring fast load times and DDoS protection by default.
</Note>
